Stone in the interior

← Back to home

We make stone for your home or flat to measure — from a kitchen worktop through a bathroom and floor to a staircase, fireplace or table. We help you choose the material, measure the slab precisely, make it in our workshop and install it.

Floors, paving and terrazzo

Staircases and fireplaces

Stone furniture

  • Bar and reception counters

Materials

We work with granite, marble, travertine, sandstone and terrazzo. On request we also offer compact slabs (ceramic and composite). For every material we advise where it suits and how to care for it — granite withstands the most in a kitchen, while marble is ideal for a bathroom or fireplace.

You can see the stone in person. We choose specific slabs for a kitchen or interior from the warehouses of our suppliers, which we monitor regularly. Book a visit and you can see the chosen slab with your own eyes before production.

Send us the dimensions or a sketch, or we can come and measure. We will prepare a free, no-obligation quotation.

FAQ

What you ask us

Which stone should I choose for a kitchen worktop?

Granite is the most durable — it withstands heat, scratches and common kitchen acids. Marble and travertine are softer and more sensitive to vinegar or citrus, so they suit a bathroom or fireplace better. On request we also offer compact slabs (ceramic and composite). More on the Stone in the interior page.

When do you measure a kitchen worktop?

Only after the kitchen units have been installed, so the slab fits the actual dimensions exactly. For an indicative quotation, a sketch with dimensions and the type of sink and hob is enough.

How do I care for a stone worktop or floor?

Warm water and a neutral cleaning product, without acids or abrasives, is enough. We seal the stone after installation; depending on use, the sealing is best renewed after some time. We can regrind and repolish a dull or scratched floor, including terrazzo.
