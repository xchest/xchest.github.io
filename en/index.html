About us

A tradition you can rely on

Kamenictví Procházka is a family firm with a tradition reaching back to 1929. For generations we have practised the stonemason's craft with an emphasis on quality, detail and a personal approach.

Every commission matters to us – we know our work has lasting value. We will help you with the choice, the design and the execution, exactly as you imagine it.
