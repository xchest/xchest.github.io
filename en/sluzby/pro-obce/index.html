For municipalities

← Back to home

We offer towns and municipalities comprehensive stonework for public places of remembrance — war memorials, plaques, tombs, and the preparation of a project and documents for a grant.

War memorials

  • Renovation of memorials to the fallen — cleaning, repairs, replacement of missing parts
  • Restoration, deepening and regilding of lettering
  • Dismantling, new foundations and reinstallation of taken-down memorials
  • Consultation and help with grant applications

Plaques and new memorials

  • Plaques for buildings and for open ground
  • Monuments, statues and stone sculptures
  • Stone crosses and wayside shrines, and their restoration
  • Company, donor and dedication plaques
  • Stone plates with house numbers and addresses

Tombs and places of remembrance

  • Renovation and repair of tombs
  • Clearance of graves
  • Construction of municipal tombs

Grants and project preparation

  • On-site inspection and a proposed scope of restoration
  • Budget and documents for a grant application
  • Preparation of the project and the work schedule
  • Before-and-after photo documentation for grant accounting

We will gladly agree with you on the scope and schedule of the work and help with the grant administration too. Get in touch for a free, no-obligation consultation.

FAQ

What you ask us

Do you help municipalities restore memorials?

Yes. We renovate memorials to the fallen, including consultations and arranging grants, repair tombs and build municipal tombs. More on the pages Memorials and plaques and For municipalities.

Do you also renovate old graves and tombs?

Yes — from cleaning and restoring lettering, through levelling sunken kerbs, to the complete reconstruction of a tomb. You will find examples under grave renovations.
