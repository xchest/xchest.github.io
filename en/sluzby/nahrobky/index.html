Gravestones and renovation

← Back to home

We design, make and install new gravestones, and restore a dignified appearance to old ones. We help you choose the material and shape and take care of the whole project, from the first design to installation at the cemetery.

What we provide

  • Designs, production and installation of single graves, double graves and urn graves
  • A choice of quality granites and natural stone in various shades and finishes
  • Added inscriptions and additions to existing gravestones
  • Engraved portraits and photos, sandblasting and restoration of lettering
  • Cemetery accessories — vases, lanterns, cover slabs and more
  • Professional installation on site

Grave renovation

  • Restoration of older graves, cleaning and removal of dirt and moss
  • Repairs and complete reconstruction of damaged parts
  • Chiselling, sandblasting and restoration of lettering
  • Levelling and stabilising of sunken or tilted kerbs
  • Replacement of missing elements and cemetery accessories

We assess the condition of the grave on site and propose the scope of work and an indicative price. We work with care and respect for the original design.

Every commission matters to us — we know our work has lasting value. We will gladly prepare a free, no-obligation quotation for you.

FAQ

What you ask us

How much does a new gravestone cost?

The price depends on the type of grave, the chosen stone, the dimensions and the inscription. You can get an indicative idea in our online designer; we prepare an exact, no-obligation quotation after a consultation.

How long do production and installation take?

It depends on the material, the availability of the stone and the season. We give you a specific date in the quotation before you place the order.

Do I need the cemetery administration's consent to alter a grave?

Alterations to a burial plot are governed by the cemetery rules and the consent of its administrator. We will advise you on what is needed and how to proceed.

Can you add an inscription to an existing gravestone?

Yes. We add names and dates, restore and regild lettering, and also do sandblasting, engraved portraits and photographs.
