Construction work

← Back to home

We carry out stone construction work – from smaller repairs to larger projects. We take care of everything from preparation to the final finish.

What we can help with

  • Stone cladding, paving and steps
  • Window sills, skirtings and other stone building elements
  • Repairs and reconstruction of stone structures
  • Preparation, transport and professional installation

Exterior

  • Terraces and outdoor paving, paving of public spaces
  • Outdoor staircases and entrance areas, anti-slip finishes
  • Low walls, plinths, cover slabs and wall copings
  • Outdoor sills, reveals and facade cladding, including ventilated facades

Stone restoration

  • Cleaning and sealing of stone facades, staircases and floors
  • Grinding and polishing of floors, restoration of terrazzo
  • Repair of cracks, replacement of broken edges and missing pieces
  • Replacement of damaged tiles and steps with matching stone

FAQ

What you ask us

How do I care for a stone worktop or floor?

Warm water and a neutral cleaning product, without acids or abrasives, is enough. We seal the stone after installation; depending on use, the sealing is best renewed after some time. We can regrind and repolish a dull or scratched floor, including terrazzo.
